@DESC@

Installation
1. Open "@APP@.dmg" and drag "@APP@" to the Applications folder.
2. The app is not notarized by Apple, so the first launch says it can't be opened. Click
   "Done", then System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway", and
   confirm with your password. (On macOS 14 and earlier you can also right-click the app >
   Open > Open.)
To uninstall, drag the app to the Trash.
